Diva&Dude Wellness

Wellness

Diva & Dude is ThriveWell Cancer Foundation’s free wellness program for cancer patients and survivors. Dozens of free classes every week across five San Antonio locations — exercise, nutrition, artful healing, and support groups — all built for life during and after treatment, and all at no cost.

Wellness at ThriveWell Cancer Foundation has a name: Diva & Dude. Diva (for women) and Dude (for men) offer the same free classes — exercise, nutrition, artful healing, and support groups — built to improve the odds of prevention and survivorship while giving you community for the road. Every class is free and open to cancer patients and survivors.

Wellness

Exercise

Movement during and after treatment can meaningfully reduce recurrence risk, ease the side effects of chemotherapy and hormone therapy, rebuild strength and range of motion, and lift self-image — led by instructors who understand the cancer journey. Classes vary by location and include:

  • Yoga & Tai Chi
  • Zumba & Aqua Zumba
  • Aerobics & Spin
  • Pilates & Nia
  • Strength & Bootcamp
  • Belly Dancing & Boundless

Nutrition

Weekly nutrition classes make healthy eating practical during and after treatment — part of the same evidence base showing that diet and movement can lower recurrence risk. Sessions cover:

  • How diet and exercise work together in prevention and healing
  • Low-saturated-fat eating, and how sugar affects cancer cells
  • Phytonutrients, antioxidants, and the vitamin D connection
  • Which foods to limit — and satisfying replacements
  • Sorting fact from fiction on the myths and hot topics
  • A trusted place to get your questions answered

Artful Healing

Guided creative sessions where the point isn’t the finished piece — it’s the release, and the people beside you. No experience needed, and there’s a way in for everyone:

  • Painting & visual arts
  • Sculpting & jewelry making
  • Storytelling & poetry

Wellness

Support Groups

Professionally facilitated groups for patients and caregivers, meeting at the same time in separate rooms so everyone can speak freely. Every session is led by a licensed facilitator.

For patients

A confidential space to share what treatment is really like — with people walking the same road.

For caregivers

Caring for someone with cancer is its own weight. This group is just for the people doing it.

Groups meet the first Wednesday of every month at 6:00 PM at The Neighborhood Place, 3014 Rivas St, San Antonio, TX 78228. An RSVP helps facilitators plan — email info@thrivewell.org or call (210) 593-5949.

Join Us

Find a Class & Join

With dozens of classes a week across five locations, the lineup changes often — so the live schedule lives in the Diva & Dude member app and the weekly e-newsletter, both yours the moment you join. Because classes are tailored to where you are in treatment, joining is a quick, one-time sign-up with a physician sign-off and activity waiver — a few minutes, no cost, no catch.
