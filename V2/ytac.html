About ThriveWell Cancer Foundation

YTAC — Young Texans Against Cancer

Young professionals, throwing great events for the best reason there is. Young Texans Against Cancer – San Antonio (YTAC), powered by ThriveWell Cancer Foundation, is where the next generation joins the fight — golf, fishing, Movember mustaches, and a lot of fun that adds up to real help for patients.

It started in 2001 with 44 young people in Houston, all connected by the same thread — some had faced cancer themselves, others had watched someone they love go through it. A San Antonio chapter joined in 2009, and in 2019 YTAC – San Antonio became part of the ThriveWell Cancer Foundation family. The idea has never changed: bring great people together, have a genuinely good time, and turn that energy into help for patients and their families.
