Legacy

Planned Giving

A planned gift lets you make a lasting difference for San Antonio families — often without affecting your finances today. Include ThriveWell Cancer Foundation in your will, living trust, or retirement plan, and help patients THRIVE for years to come.

To start a conversation, call (210) 593-5949 or email impact@thrivewell.org.

Tax-Smart

Give From Your IRA

If you are 70½ or older, you can give directly from your IRA to ThriveWell Cancer Foundation — a qualified charitable distribution (QCD) — without paying income tax on the transfer. Beginning at 73, it can also count toward your required minimum distribution.
